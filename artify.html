Artify AI

An AI image generator web app that turns text prompts into stunning visuals, with a clean dashboard to create, organize and manage your art.

Turn imagination into art, in seconds

AI art tools can feel technical, with crowded settings and confusing plans. Artify AI keeps everything in one calm dashboard, so creators can generate, save and manage their images without getting lost.

  • 01
    The problemCrowded settings and unclear pricing make AI art tools hard for new users.
  • 02
    The solutionA light, airy dashboard with a purple accent, clear collections and simple plans.
  • 03
    My roleUser flows, wireframes, visual design and an interactive prototype.

How I Worked

Design Process

  1. 01

    Research

    Explored popular AI art tools and noted where new users got stuck.

  2. 02

    User Flow

    Mapped the path from signing in to writing a prompt and downloading an image.

  3. 03

    Wireframes

    Planned a sidebar dashboard layout that keeps every tool one click away.

  4. 04

    Visual Design

    Designed a light UI with a purple gradient and Outfit for all text.

  5. 05

    Prototype

    Linked the screens in Figma into a clickable prototype to test the flow.
